# Kinlo name and branding

The source code is open source under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). You may use,
modify and distribute it, including commercially, as long as you keep the
copyright notice and licence text.

The licence covers the code only. These are **not** licensed for reuse:

- The name "Kinlo" and the tagline "Financial peace begins with clarity."
- The Kinlo logo, leaf mark, app icon and colour-and-type identity as a brand
- The launch video, screenshots and other images in `docs/` and `assets/`

If you build on this code, please:

1. Keep the copyright notice and the MIT licence text in your copy.
2. Credit the original, for example: "Based on Kinlo by Mithilesh,
   https://github.com/Mithilesh9mk/kinlo-app". A credit in your README or an
   About screen is ideal.
3. Give your app its own name, icon and branding.

Questions or special cases: message the author through GitHub.
