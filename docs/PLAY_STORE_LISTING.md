# Play Store listing — draft text

Set in Play Console (Grow → Store presence → Main store listing), not in the APK.
The name under the icon on the phone stays **Kinlo** (`expo.name` in `app.json`).

## App name (30 characters max)
Kinlo: Split Expenses

## Short description (80 characters max)
Split bills with friends and groups, scan payment screenshots, track spending.

## Full description (draft)
Kinlo makes sharing costs with friends, flatmates and family simple, and keeps your own spending in one place.

SPLIT WITH PEOPLE
• Split an expense with a friend or a whole circle: equally, by exact amounts, by percentage or by shares.
• See who owes whom at a glance, and settle up when you are ready.
• "Fewest payments" turns a circle's debts into the smallest number of transfers.
• Repeating expenses: set rent or a subscription to come back every week, month or year.
• Reminders so nobody has to chase anyone.

TRACK YOUR OWN SPENDING
• Scan a payment screenshot and Kinlo fills in the amount, merchant and date for you.
• Or add a personal expense by hand in a few seconds.
• Quick-add widget on your home screen: Split, Scan or Manual in one tap.

BUILT FOR INDIA
• Amounts in rupees, with UPI payment screenshots in mind.

Kinlo Gold adds unlimited scans, unlimited reminders and CSV statement export.
