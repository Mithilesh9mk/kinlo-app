  • Repeating expenses. Rent comes back every week, month or year.

Screenshot in, expense out.

An AI service reads your UPI or bank payment screenshot into a personal expense. You check it before you rely on it. It does not create a shared expense. The image is sent to an AI service (Anthropic, or Google as a backup) to be read.

Free plan: 3 scans a month per account, 6 per app install.

A nudge that does the chasing.

Tap Send reminder on a friend who owes you. One a day, per person. Kinlo records who owes what. It does not move money.

What Kinlo does differently.

Reminders are built in
Nudge a friend who owes you, once a day. Notifications are not working in the current preview yet.
Screenshots become personal expenses
Scan a payment screenshot instead of typing it.
Friends who have not joined
Add them first. If they join later, you both confirm the link.
Open source
MIT-licensed code on GitHub. The Kinlo name and logo stay reserved.
Private by default
No ads, no tracking SDKs, no data sales. Read the privacy policy.
