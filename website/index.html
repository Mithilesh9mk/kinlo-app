Circles, friends, and who paid.

  • Circles and friends. Split with a group or one person.
  • Four ways to split. Equal, exact amounts, percentages or shares.
  • Fewer payments. A circle’s debts are netted down to fewer transfers.
  • Settle in your UPI app. Kinlo keeps the record and never holds your money.
  • Repeating expenses. Rent comes back every week, month or year.
