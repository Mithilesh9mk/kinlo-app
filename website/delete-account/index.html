Delete your Kinlo account

Version 1.0 · Effective 31 October 2026

The short version

  • Delete from the app in Profile. Deleting cannot be undone.
  • Your private data and your login are erased.
  • Expenses you shared with other people stay, with your name replaced by “Deleted user”.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Kinlo and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Data & Export Compliance.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Read what will be removed and what will stay.
  5. Type DELETE, then tap Delete forever on the final confirmation.

Outstanding balances do not block deletion, and the app shows them before you confirm. If deleting stops partway, your private data may already be gone. Try again from the app. If it still fails, email us at the address at the foot of this page.

If you cannot open the app

Email us from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Kinlo account”. Use the email address at the foot of this page. We will reply to confirm and aim to finish within 30 days. Never send your password.

What is deleted

  • Your login: your email address, password and Google link.
  • Your name, phone number, avatar, UPI ID and notification token. Your profile becomes “Deleted user”.
  • Your personal spending entries, reminders and unposted drafts.
  • Your friend links, and reminder records you sent or received.
  • Your repeating expense templates, plan, coupon and scan records, and usage counters.
  • Your scanned payment screenshots in private storage. We remove them in batches. If a removal fails, the rest of the deletion still goes ahead and we log how many files failed, so you can email us to check.
  • People you added who have not joined and who have no expense history.

The login is removed last. If that step fails, the plan, coupon, scan and usage records tied to it can remain until we finish the deletion with you by email.

What stays

Expenses you shared are also other people’s records, and deleting your account must not change their balances. So these stay, with your identity removed:

  • Shared expenses and their amounts, who paid, each person’s share and settlements.
  • Descriptions you wrote on shared expenses, circle memberships and activity entries.
  • People you added who have expense history. They keep the name and number you entered, because those expenses still need them.
  • Anything you typed into a description or note on a shared expense stays as written, including any names in it.
  • A record of which accounts matched when someone looked up a phone number, and records of connection requests between you and others. Your account row keeps no name, phone number or UPI ID.
  • Backup copies at Supabase, until they are overwritten. We do not control exactly when.

If you still owe money or are owed money, deleting does not cancel it. The other person keeps their record against a participant with no name.

After deleting

You cannot get a deleted account back. You can sign up again, but it will be a new account with no history. To see what we hold about you before you decide, read the privacy policy.
